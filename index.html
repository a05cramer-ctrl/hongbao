The rules

Fixed at launch. Nothing here changes after the coin goes live.

  • WhenEvery hour at :00 UTC, 24 envelopes a day.
  • Winners88 wallets per envelope, one ticket per wallet, no matter how big the bag.
  • TicketHold at least 100,000 $HONGBAO (0.01% of supply) when the hour turns.
  • Not eligibleBonding curve, pool, burn and dev wallets.
  • Seedsha256(blockhash ‖ hour ‖ mint) from the first block after :00.
  • SplitEach grab is random between 0.0001 SOL and twice the average left. Last grab takes the rest.
  • MinimumUnder 0.088 SOL, the envelope rolls over to the next hour.
  • Fees88% to the envelope, 12% buys and burns $HONGBAO.
  • Fewer than 88If fewer than 88 wallets qualify, every one of them gets an envelope.
  • Paid inSOL, sent straight to the winning wallets. No claim, no sign-up.
